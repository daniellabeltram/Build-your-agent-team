# Employment & payroll compliance

*Roster skill S13. Preloaded into: `florida-compliance-advisor`, `royalties-accounting-tax-specialist`, `talent-org-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Hire and pay people (and authors) correctly.

## Key rules (docs/05, verify current thresholds)
- **Federal:** EIN, Form 941 quarterly, W-2s, Form 940 FUTA, and new-hire reporting to the Florida New Hire Reporting Center.
- **Florida reemployment tax:** register with FL DOR (RT-6 quarterly reports).
- **Workers' compensation:** required for non-construction employers with **4+ employees** (part-time included).
- **I-9** for every hire. **E-Verify is required for private employers with 25+ employees** (FL SB 1718, 2023).
- **No state income tax** in Florida, which simplifies withholding.
- **Classification:** freelance editors and designers are usually contractors. Apply the IRS common-law test and avoid controlling how, when, and where they work. Issue **1099-NEC** to contractors paid ≥ the IRS threshold.
- **Author royalties** are reported on **1099-MISC, Box 2**. Collect a W-9 at signing (W-8BEN for foreign authors, with treaty withholding considerations).
- Remote employees in other states trigger that state's payroll registration and income tax withholding.

## Method
1. Identify the worker type and location.
2. Produce the registration and onboarding checklist and the recurring filing calendar.
3. Recommend a payroll provider (e.g. Gusto/ADP) and the right setup.

## Boundary
Confirm with a CPA or employment attorney for edge cases.
