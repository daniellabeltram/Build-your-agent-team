## Scoring
Likelihood 1–5 × Impact 1–5 = score (≥15 red, 8–14 amber, ≤7 green). Note velocity (how fast it hits) and detectability.

## Method
1. Define the scope (the whole venture, a decision, a title, or a contract).
2. Brainstorm risks across categories: financial, market, operational, legal, people, technology, reputational, and environmental.
3. Score them, then rank.
4. For each red or amber risk: mitigation (avoid, reduce, transfer, accept), owner (agent/role), **early-warning indicator**, and **trigger action**.
5. Note how the risk changes the recommendation under review.

## Output
A risk table: ID, risk, L, I, score, mitigation, owner, indicator, trigger. Then 3 headline risks in plain English.
