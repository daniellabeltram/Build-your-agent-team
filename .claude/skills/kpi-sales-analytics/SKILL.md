# KPI design & sales analytics

*Roster skill S33. Preloaded into: `brand-marketing-strategist`, `digital-social-marketer`, `finance-capital-planner`, `sales-distribution-manager`, `trade-library-marketing-specialist`.*

## Purpose
Make decisions from data: reprint or not, more marketing or stop, which channels and imprints to grow.

## Core KPIs (docs/07)
- **Title:** preorders; initial sell-in by account; weekly sell-through (POS, e.g. BookScan, plus distributor/Amazon data); **sell-through rate** = net sales ÷ shipped; **return rate**; weeks of inventory; earn-out status.
- **Marketing:** review count (trade and consumer), media hits, newsletter growth and open rates, social followers, **ROAS/ACoS** per paid channel, BookBub ROI.
- **List/house:** revenue by imprint and format, frontlist vs. backlist mix, gross margin per title, % of titles profitable, agent submissions per month (brand strength proxy).
- **Finance:** cash runway, months to break even, receivables aging, and the returns reserve balance.

## Method
1. Tie each KPI to a decision and an owner, and set a target and a threshold.
2. Identify data sources and refresh cadence (weekly during launch, monthly for the house).
3. Analyze: compare against plan and comps, and break down by channel and format. Separate sell-in from sell-through.
4. Recommend an action (reprint, promote, cut ads, change price) with expected impact.

## Output
KPI definitions table, dashboard spec, and diagnostic memos.
