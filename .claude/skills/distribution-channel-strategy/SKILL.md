# Distribution & channel strategy

*Roster skill S15. Preloaded into: `finance-capital-planner`, `publishing-strategy-advisor`, `sales-distribution-manager`, `special-sales-d2c-manager`, `trade-library-marketing-specialist`.*

## Purpose
Distribution is the gatekeeper to bookstores and libraries (docs/08 risk #3).

## Options (est.)
| Option | Fit | Typical terms |
|---|---|---|
| **IngramSpark** (POD + wholesale listing) | Day 1, any size | Set discount (30–55%) and returnability. No sales reps |
| Smaller distributors / aggregators | 1–10 titles | Fees vary. Some sales rep coverage |
| **IPG, PGW (Ingram Publisher Services family), Consortium**, etc. | ~10+ titles or a strong track record | ~25–30% of net receipts. Sales reps, catalogs, warehousing |
| **PRH Publisher Services / S&S Distribution** | Established lists | Big Five sales force. Selective |

## Channel mix (docs/02, est.)
Amazon 40–55%, B&N 10–20%, indies 10–20%, libraries 5–15%, special sales 5–20%, direct-to-consumer 2–10%.

## Method
1. Define needs: title count per season, formats, sales rep coverage, warehousing, international, and library reach.
2. Score the distributors on reach, fees, reserves and payment terms, minimums, exclusivity, tech/data, and exit terms.
3. Model the net margin per channel (the discount plus fees plus returns).
4. Draft a **3-season path:** e.g. Season 1–2 IngramSpark plus direct special sales → Season 3+ a full-service distributor application with sales history.
5. Set the policy: discounts, returnability, short-discount exceptions, and non-returnable special sales.

## Output
A comparison matrix, recommended path with milestones, and channel policy.

## Pitfalls
- Exclusive distribution contracts with long terms and inventory lock-in.
- Non-returnable trade discounts mean bookstores won't stock the books.
