# Intellectual property management

*Roster skill S11. Preloaded into: `ai-emerging-tech-advisor`, `digital-publishing-metadata-specialist`, `media-law-counsel`, `publishing-contracts-attorney`.*

## Purpose
Protect what we own and avoid infringing what others own.

## Checklists (docs/05)
**Trademarks (USPTO):** knockout search (USPTO search, state register, Sunbiz names, domain and social handles) → file for the house and imprint names and logos in **Class 16** (printed books), **Class 9** (ebooks/audio), and **Class 41** (publishing services). Est. ~$350 per class per mark plus attorney fees. Never use "Penguin," "Random House," or any PRH imprint names or marks.
**Copyright:** register each work via eCO (~$45–65), usually in the author's name. Send the **mandatory deposit** of 2 copies to the Copyright Office. Get Library of Congress PCN/CIP where eligible.
**Permissions:** build a permissions log per title (item, rights holder, scope, territory, formats, fee, credit line, status). Song lyrics are costly and slow, so cut them where possible. Images need model and property releases if people or private property are identifiable.
**Fair use:** apply the four factors (purpose, nature, amount, market effect). Short quotations for criticism or commentary are usually OK, but treat epigraphs and lyrics cautiously. When unsure, seek permission or cut.

## Method
1. Identify the IP asset or risk.
2. Apply the checklist and note gaps.
3. Recommend action (file, license, cut, or get an attorney opinion) with cost and timeline.

## Boundary
Flag anything contentious for a licensed IP attorney.
