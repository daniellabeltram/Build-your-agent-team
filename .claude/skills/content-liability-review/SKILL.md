# Content & media-liability risk review

*Roster skill S14. Preloaded into: `editorial-acquisitions-director`, `media-law-counsel`, `publishing-contracts-attorney`, `risk-insurance-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Catch legal and reputational landmines before they cost money.

## Red flags to scan for
1. **Defamation:** false statements of fact about identifiable living people or companies, especially in memoir, business, and exposé books. The truth defense needs documentation.
2. **Privacy:** public disclosure of private facts, false light, and intrusion. Health, sexual, financial, and family details about non-public people are high risk.
3. **Right of publicity:** using a real person's name or likeness to sell (covers, marketing).
4. **Permissions:** quotes, lyrics, images, maps, and charts (see ip-management).
5. **Fiction "roman à clef":** characters recognizable as real people.
6. **Florida book-challenge exposure:** HB 1069 school-library challenge process. In *PRH v. Gibson* (Aug 2025) the removal provisions were ruled unconstitutional, but appeals and new laws are possible. This affects **school-library sales of children's and YA titles**, not retail.
7. **Advice liability:** health, legal, and financial guidance needs disclaimers and expert vetting.

## Method
1. Classify the risk level of the title (Low / Medium / High) from its genre and content.
2. For Medium/High: list each flagged passage, the risk type, and a mitigation (verify, anonymize, cut, get consent, add a disclaimer, or seek legal review).
3. Confirm author warranties and indemnities, and **media-liability (E&O) insurance** coverage.
4. Record the decision in a vetting memo.

## Boundary
High-risk items go to licensed media counsel. This skill triages; it does not clear content.
