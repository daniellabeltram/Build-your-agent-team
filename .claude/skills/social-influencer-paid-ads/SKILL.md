# Social, influencer & paid advertising

*Roster skill S26. Preloaded into: `brand-marketing-strategist`, `digital-social-marketer`, `special-sales-d2c-manager`.*

## Purpose
Digital discovery drives breakouts, especially in genre fiction (docs/01, docs/07).

## Playbooks
- **BookTok/Bookstagram:** seed 20–100 relevant creators with ARCs or finished copies (gifted, no pay obligation, with FTC disclosure for paid partnerships). Supply hooks (tropes, aesthetic, quotes). Repost UGC.
- **Newsletters:** a list per imprint and per author. Lead magnets (excerpts, bonus chapters). Cadence: monthly plus launch.
- **Amazon Ads:** sponsored products on comps and author/keyword targets. Start at $10–30/day per title, track ACoS against royalty margin, and cut losers weekly.
- **BookBub:** Featured Deals for ebook price promotions (backlist and series starters). BookBub Ads for targeting.
- **Meta/Instagram ads:** retarget and promote lookalikes for lead titles and events.

## Method
1. Set the objective (preorders, launch rank, backlist revenue, list growth) and a target CPA (cost per acquisition) from unit economics.
2. Choose channels by persona (Genre Binge Reader → TikTok/BookBub; Ambitious Professional → LinkedIn/podcasts; Book Club Member → newsletters/Facebook).
3. Launch small, measure weekly, scale winners, and cut losers.
4. Report the ROI per channel (kpi-sales-analytics).

## Pitfalls
- Paying for reach without tracking conversions.
- Breaking FTC disclosure rules.
