# Publicity & media relations

*Roster skill S25. Preloaded into: `brand-marketing-strategist`, `editorial-acquisitions-director`, `publicity-community-relations-lead`.*

## Purpose
Earned media builds credibility and sells nonfiction and upmarket fiction.

## Assets
Press release, author Q&A, pitch angles (news hooks, local Florida angles, seasonal tie-ins), author bio and photo, excerpt, talking points, and an ARC request form.

## Media tiers (docs/07)
- **National:** trade reviews (PW, Kirkus, LJ, Booklist), national print and online, podcasts, TV.
- **Florida/local:** *Palm Beach Post*, *Palm Beach Daily News*, *Palm Beach Illustrated*, *Boca Raton* magazine, *Sun Sentinel*, *Miami Herald*, WLRN, WPTV/WPBF.
- **Niche:** category newsletters, Substacks, trade and association publications (business books).
- **House PR:** Publishers Lunch/Marketplace deal announcements, PW news for launches and hires.

## Method
1. Find 3–5 angles per title and match them to outlets and lead times (monthlies 4–6 months, weeklies 4–8 weeks, broadcast 2–4 weeks).
2. Personalize pitches (≤150 words, with the angle first), follow up once, and track in the CRM.
3. Media-train the author (key messages, bridging, a 30-second answer).
4. For a crisis (controversy, challenge, error): get the facts, appoint one spokesperson, issue a holding statement within hours, coordinate with legal (media-law counsel), and correct transparently.

## Output
Media list, pitch drafts, press kit, publicity calendar, and coverage report.
