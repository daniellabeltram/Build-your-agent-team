# Florida & local regulatory compliance

*Roster skill S12. Preloaded into: `florida-compliance-advisor`, `operations-continuity-manager`, `royalties-accounting-tax-specialist`, `talent-org-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Keep the company legally in good standing with minimal friction.

## Requirements (docs/05; verify current fees before filing)
| Item | Agency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LLC or corporation | Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) | ~$125 LLC. **Annual report Jan 1–May 1** (~$138.75; $400 late fee) |
| Fictitious name (DBA) per imprint | Sunbiz | ~$50, advertise first, renew every 5 years |
| EIN | IRS | Free |
| **County Local Business Tax Receipt** | PBC Tax Collector (pbctax.gov) | Required even for home-based and one-person businesses. Renew by Sept 30 |
| **City of WPB Business Tax Receipt** | WPB Development Services | Required in addition to the county BTR inside city limits. **Home Occupation Affidavit** if home-based |
| Zoning / Certificate of Use | WPB Zoning (PBC Zoning Form 103 if unincorporated) | Confirm office and inventory use |
| Sales & use tax (DR-1) | Florida DOR | For direct consumer sales. 6% state + PBC surtax (verify; ~1%). Collect resale certificates from retailers |
| Remote-state sales tax | Other states | Economic nexus thresholds (often $100k) |

## Method
1. Confirm the facts (entity type, address inside or outside city limits, home vs. commercial, direct sales?).
2. Produce an **ordered checklist** with agency, form, fee (verified via the web-research-citation skill), deadline, and owner.
3. Add a **compliance calendar** (annual report, BTR renewals, sales tax returns, 1099s).

## Boundary
Confirm with a Florida CPA or attorney. Fees and rates change, so always re-verify them on the official site.
