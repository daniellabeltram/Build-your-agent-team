# Publishing contract drafting & review

*Roster skill S10. Preloaded into: `audio-publishing-producer`, `editorial-acquisitions-director`, `publishing-contracts-attorney`, `royalties-accounting-tax-specialist`, `sales-distribution-manager`, `subsidiary-rights-specialist`, `talent-org-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Contracts define the company's assets (the rights it owns). Get the structure right and flag issues for a licensed Florida attorney.

## Author agreement: key clauses checklist
1. **Grant of rights:** formats (print, ebook, audio), territory (World / World English / North America), language, and term (life of copyright vs. a fixed term).
2. **Advance and payout** schedule (signing / delivery & acceptance / publication).
3. **Royalties** by format, with escalators (see title-pl-unit-economics), the high-discount clause, and the ebook rate on net.
4. **Subsidiary rights** splits (book club, paperback reprint, translation, film/TV, audio license, serial).
5. **Delivery and acceptance** (a "satisfactory in form and content" standard) and revision rounds.
6. **Warranties and indemnities:** originality, non-infringement, non-libelous, and **no AI-generated content without disclosure** (see ai-policy-governance).
7. **Publication obligation** (within X months) and approvals (cover/title consultation).
8. **Option clause** (narrow: next work, same genre, exclusive look period).
9. **Out-of-print / reversion:** define it by sales threshold, not just availability (POD makes "in print" meaningless).
10. **Accounting:** semiannual statements, audit rights, and the reserve against returns (cap ~20–25%, liquidated within 2–3 periods).
11. **Termination, bankruptcy, assignment** (allow assignment on sale of the company: important for an exit).
12. **Copyright registration** in the author's name, handled by the publisher.

## Other agreements
- **Freelancers:** work-for-hire plus a copyright assignment backup, confidentiality, and deliverables/payment terms.
- **Distribution:** exclusivity, fee %, returns reserve, payment terms, term/termination, inventory ownership, and the exit clause.
- **Printing:** specs, overs/unders tolerance, delivery, and paper price clauses.
- **Audio / translation licenses:** term, territory, advance and royalty, and approvals.

## Method
Produce: (1) a clause-by-clause summary in plain English, (2) a red/amber/green issue list, (3) proposed redlines or a draft, (4) **"Attorney review required"** items.

## Boundary
This is not legal advice. Always route the final language to licensed counsel.
