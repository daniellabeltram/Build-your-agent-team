# Organization design & recruiting

*Roster skill S22. Preloaded into: `operations-continuity-manager`, `publishing-strategy-advisor`, `talent-org-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Hire for taste and relationships, and outsource everything else (docs/06).

## Benchmarks
- PW 2025: median publishing pay **$80,000**. PW 2026: average **$57,100** for ≤6 years of experience *(sourced)*.
- Ranges (est.): Editorial Director $90–150k. Senior Editor $65–100k. Assistant Editor $48–62k. Managing Editor $65–95k. Marketing Director $75–120k. Publicist $50–80k. Sales & Rights $70–110k. Add **20–30%** for taxes and benefits.
- Florida has no state income tax and a lower cost of living than NYC. Remote-first widens the talent pool.

## Phases (docs/06)
- **Phase 0 (months 0–6):** Founder + **Editorial Director** + freelance bench.
- **Phase 1 (Year 1, 4–6 titles):** + Managing Editor/Production, + Marketing & Publicity Manager (3–4 FTE).
- **Phase 2 (Years 2–3, 8–15 titles):** + acquiring editors per imprint, editorial assistants, Sales & Rights, digital marketing, ops/royalties (6–9 FTE).

## Method
1. Map the work to roles and decide in-house vs. freelance vs. outsourced (distributor, printer, payroll).
2. Tie each hire to a trigger (title count, revenue, or workload).
3. Write role scorecards (mission, outcomes, competencies) and comp bands.
4. Source: PW Jobzone, Bookjobs, Publishers Lunch, publishing institutes (Columbia, NYU, Denver), FAU/PBAU/Lynn, the NYC diaspora.
5. Plan retention for key people (equity or profit share for the editorial director; key-person risk, docs/08 #7).

## Output
Org chart by phase, hiring timeline with triggers, scorecards, and comp bands with total cost.
