# Insurance & business continuity

*Roster skill S32. Preloaded into: `florida-compliance-advisor`, `operations-continuity-manager`, `risk-insurance-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Transfer the risks we can't afford, and keep publishing through disruptions.

## Coverage checklist (docs/04–05, est. $3k–$10k/yr to start)
| Policy | Why |
|---|---|
| **Media liability / publishers' E&O** | Libel, privacy, copyright/trademark infringement claims. **Essential** |
| General liability | Events, office visitors |
| Property / contents | Office equipment (flood is separate in FL: check NFIP/private flood) |
| **Business interruption** | Hurricane closure, lost income |
| Cyber | Customer data (D2C store), ransomware |
| Workers' comp | Required at 4+ employees in FL |
| D&O | If outside investors or a board |
| Inventory/transit | Usually covered by the distributor and printer. Confirm |

## Hurricane continuity (season Jun 1–Nov 30)
1. **No inventory in WPB.** Use the distributor's warehouse out of state.
2. Everything is cloud-based (files, accounting, email). Laptops travel with staff.
3. Remote-capable team, with a contact tree and check-in protocol.
4. Pre-season checklist (May): backups verified, insurance docs saved, vendor contacts, and the office secured.
5. Communicate schedule impacts to the distributor, printers, and authors early.

## Method
Map the exposures → choose the coverage and limits → get 2–3 broker quotes → review exclusions → write the continuity plan and test it once a year.
