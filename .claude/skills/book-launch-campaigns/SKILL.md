# Book launch & campaign planning

*Roster skill S24. Preloaded into: `brand-marketing-strategist`, `digital-social-marketer`, `publicity-community-relations-lead`, `special-sales-d2c-manager`, `trade-library-marketing-specialist`.*

## Purpose
A repeatable launch playbook so every title gets a professional campaign sized to its potential.

## Tiering
- **Lead titles (1–2 per season):** most of the budget ($10k–$25k, est.), a publicist, a tour/events, and paid media.
- **Midlist:** $2k–$7.5k (trade reviews, social, targeted events).
- **Backlist revival:** price promotions, BookBub, seasonal tie-ins.
Rule of thumb: spend **10–15% of projected net revenue** on marketing (docs/07).

## Timeline (T = publication date)
| When | Actions |
|---|---|
| T−9 to −6 mo | Metadata final, catalog copy, positioning, comp titles, sales-conference pitch |
| T−6 to −4 mo | ARCs out (NetGalley/Edelweiss), trade review submissions (PW, Kirkus, LJ, Booklist), blurbs, preorder campaign plan |
| T−4 to −2 mo | Influencer seeding, media pitching (long-lead), event booking, library marketing, bookseller outreach |
| T−2 to 0 | Preorder push, newsletter, ads warm-up, launch event, local press |
| T to T+3 mo | Short-lead media, reviews, ads optimization, book clubs, awards submissions, sell-through tracking |

## Method
1. Set the tier and budget from the title P&L.
2. Map personas → channels → messages → assets.
3. Build a RACI across agents (publicity, digital, trade/library, special sales).
4. Define KPIs (kpi-sales-analytics) and review weekly from T−8 weeks.

## Output
Launch plan table with dates, owners, budget lines, and KPIs.
