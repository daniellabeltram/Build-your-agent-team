# Commercial negotiation

*Roster skill S34. Preloaded into: `editorial-acquisitions-director`, `operations-continuity-manager`, `production-manufacturing-manager`, `sales-distribution-manager`, `subsidiary-rights-specialist`.*

## Purpose
Small houses win on preparation, not leverage.

## Prep sheet (fill in before every negotiation)
1. **Objective:** what success looks like (must-haves vs. nice-to-haves).
2. **BATNA:** our best alternative if there's no deal, and our estimate of theirs.
3. **Walk-away point** (reservation value) and **target**.
4. **Their interests:** what they need (agents: advance size, marketing commitment, speed; distributors: title volume, quality; printers: steady volume; landlords: term length, credit).
5. **Tradeables:** low cost to us, high value to them (e.g. faster pub date, cover consultation, longer term, prepayment, volume commitment).
6. **Market benchmarks:** from the relevant skill (distributor fees, royalty norms, rent per sq ft: WPB Class B ~$25–31/sf, Class A ~$48–72/sf).
7. **Opening position** and concession plan (give slowly, get something for everything).

## Method
1. Complete the prep sheet with the subject-matter agent.
2. Script the opening and key asks.
3. Debrief: record terms and lessons in the CRM/notes.

## Pitfalls
- Negotiating against yourself.
- Ignoring exit terms (distributor term, lease break clause, option clause).
