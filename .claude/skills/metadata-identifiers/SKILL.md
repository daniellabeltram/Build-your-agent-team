# Metadata & identifier management

*Roster skill S17. Preloaded into: `digital-publishing-metadata-specialist`, `digital-social-marketer`, `publishing-technology-architect`, `sales-distribution-manager`, `trade-library-marketing-specialist`.*

## Purpose
Bad metadata is the hidden killer of indie sales (docs/07).

## Identifiers
- **ISBN:** Bowker is the only US agency. 10 for $295, **100 for $575**, 1,000 for $1,500 *(sourced 2026)*. One ISBN per format (HC, PB, ebook, audio). The publisher of record goes on the ISBN.
- **Barcode:** EAN-13 with a 5-digit price add-on (e.g. 52800 = $28.00). Generate it for free rather than paying $25.

## Metadata checklist per title
1. Title and subtitle (the subtitle carries keywords for nonfiction)
2. Contributors with roles and short bios
3. **BISAC** codes (up to 3, most specific first)
4. Up to 7 retailer keywords (search phrases readers use)
5. Description: hook line, 150–300 words, and an author bio
6. **Comp titles** (recent, same audience)
7. Price by territory, publication and on-sale dates, format specs, page count, trim
8. Age/grade range (children's), series name and number
9. Cover image (high-res), sample pages, and review quotes as they arrive
10. Accessibility metadata for ebooks (see accessible-digital-formats)

## Method
Set metadata **6–9 months before publication** (it feeds catalogs and Edelweiss). Validate the ONIX 3.0 feed through the distributor. Audit live retail pages at launch and quarterly.

## Pitfalls
- Changing ISBNs after metadata is distributed.
- Generic keywords ("novel," "book").
