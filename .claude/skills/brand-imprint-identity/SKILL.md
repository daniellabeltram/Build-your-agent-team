# Brand & imprint identity

*Roster skill S23. Preloaded into: `brand-marketing-strategist`, `editorial-acquisitions-director`, `publicity-community-relations-lead`, `publishing-strategy-advisor`.*

## Purpose
Readers rarely know publishers, but **agents, booksellers, and librarians do**. Imprints signal taste and help acquisitions.

## Brand architecture
- **House brand** (corporate, trade-facing): a credible traditional publisher with Big Five standards and boutique attention, and **no author fees** (positioned against local hybrids).
- **Imprints** (2–3 to start, docs/README): e.g. Florida & coastal regional nonfiction; business, leadership & finance; upmarket/commercial fiction with Florida settings.
- Each imprint needs: a name, a one-line promise, target personas (customer-segmentation), category list, visual identity (colophon, cover design principles), and acquisition criteria.

## Naming method
1. Generate 20–40 candidates (place, nature, literary, and abstract themes). Avoid anything evoking PRH marks ("Penguin," "Random," "Knopf," "Viking," etc.).
2. Screen: pronounceable, spellable, works as a colophon, no negative meanings, and available on **USPTO, Sunbiz, .com/.press domains, and social handles** (ip-management skill).
3. Shortlist 3–5 per brand and test them with agents and booksellers.

## Output
Brand architecture diagram, imprint one-pagers, naming shortlist with clearance status, and a designer brief.
