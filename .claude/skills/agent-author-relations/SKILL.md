# Agent & author relations

*Roster skill S09. Preloaded into: `editorial-acquisitions-director`, `publicity-community-relations-lead`, `publishing-contracts-attorney`, `subsidiary-rights-specialist`.*

## Purpose
Agents control most quality manuscripts. Our ability to acquire depends on agents trusting us.

## What agents need to see from a new house (docs/02)
- Credible editors with taste and track records
- Real distribution into bookstores and libraries
- Competitive advances (indie range est. $2k–$25k) and standard royalties
- A marketing plan per title, on-time payments, and transparent royalty statements

## Method
1. **Target list:** identify 50–100 agents who represent our categories (use Publishers Marketplace deal reports, agency sites, and #MSWL). Track them in a simple CRM table.
2. **Introduce the house:** a short "what we're acquiring" letter per imprint with examples of comps, advance levels, and our distribution.
3. **Cadence:** a quarterly wish-list update, two NYC trips a year, and the Miami Book Fair.
4. **Submission handling:** acknowledge within 48 hours, decide within 4–6 weeks, and decline graciously.
5. **Offers:** a clear offer email (advance, payout schedule, territory, formats, royalty rates, pub season, marketing commitments).
6. **Author care:** an onboarding packet, schedule, a single point of contact, launch plan sign-off, and royalty statements on time.

## Output
Agent target lists, outreach emails, offer letters, and author onboarding checklists.

## Pitfalls
- Over-promising marketing we can't fund.
- Slow responses. Speed is our competitive edge.
