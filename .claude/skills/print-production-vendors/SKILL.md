# Print production & vendor management

*Roster skill S16. Preloaded into: `operations-continuity-manager`, `production-manufacturing-manager`, `sales-distribution-manager`.*

## Purpose
Get the right number of good-quality books to the right place, on time, at the right unit cost.

## Rules of thumb (docs/04, est.)
- A 3,000-copy offset hardcover costs ~$3–5/unit. Unit cost falls steeply with quantity.
- POD has a higher unit cost but zero inventory risk. Use it for backlist tails, test titles, and as a stock-out backup.
- Production schedule: final manuscript → copyedit (3–4 wks) → typeset → proofs (2 rounds) → files to printer **≥ 8–10 weeks before the on-sale date** for domestic offset. Color or overseas printing takes 12–16+ weeks.
- Keep relationships with **2–3 printers** (US mono, US/overseas color).

## Method
1. Write a spec sheet: trim, page count, paper (weight and shade), binding, jacket/cover finish, color, and quantity breaks.
2. RFQ at least 3 printers at 2–3 quantities and compare the landed cost (print plus freight to the distributor warehouse).
3. Choose the print run from the title P&L: the initial run should cover ~8–12 weeks of expected demand plus distributor stocking. Plan quick reprints rather than overprinting.
4. Set reprint triggers (e.g. stock < 6 weeks of sales) and lead times.
5. Track the schedule with milestone owners and buffer.

## Output
Spec sheet, quote comparison, print-run recommendation, and production schedule.

## Pitfalls
- Paper price swings and capacity crunches before Q4. Book press time early.
- Warehousing in WPB. Ship directly to the distributor's warehouse (hurricane risk, docs/08 #12).
