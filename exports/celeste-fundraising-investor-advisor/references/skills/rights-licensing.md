# Rights licensing & negotiation

## Purpose
Rights income is high margin and can turn a break-even title profitable (docs/04).

## Rights types and typical deal structure (est.)
| Right | Structure | Typical publisher share (if controlled) |
|---|---|---|
| Translation/foreign | Advance + royalty per territory, 5–7 year license | ~20–25% (author 75–80%) |
| Film/TV | Option fee (e.g. 10% of purchase price), purchase price, bonuses | Often retained by the author's agent |
| Audio license | Advance + royalty | Often 50/50 |
| Book club / large print / serial | Flat fee or royalty | 50/50 |
| AI training | License fee, scope-limited, opt-in | Requires author consent (ai-policy-governance) |

## Method
1. Audit which rights we control in each author contract.
2. Build a **rights guide** per season (synopsis, comps, sales, reviews, sample translation-ready pages).
3. Pitch to co-agents and scouts and at **Frankfurt and London Book Fairs** (and Bologna for children's).
4. Evaluate offers: advance, royalty, term, territory, formats, approvals, and payment timing.
5. Negotiate, then document with a license agreement (publishing-contracts).

## Output
Rights audit, rights guide, offer comparison, and negotiation plan.
