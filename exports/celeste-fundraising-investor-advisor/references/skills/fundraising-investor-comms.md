# Fundraising & investor communications

## Purpose
Raise the **$500k–$1.2M** (est., docs/04) needed over 3 years on acceptable terms.

## Funding sources (docs/04)
- Founder capital; **Palm Beach angels and family offices**; SBA 7(a) loans (personal guarantee, collateral, business plan); revenue-based financing once there's a backlist; a **nonprofit sister imprint** (grants: NEA, state humanities council, foundations); Palm Beach County / Florida small-business resources (BDB, SBDC).

## Pitch deck outline (10–12 slides)
1. Problem/opportunity (mid-list gap, Sun Belt growth, Florida market)
2. Solution and model (traditional, PRH-style independent house)
3. Market (AAP $21.7B trade; local demographics)
4. Imprint strategy and first list
5. Competitive positioning
6. Go-to-market (distribution path, marketing)
7. Team (editorial director pedigree)
8. Financials (3-year plan, title economics, path to breakeven)
9. Risks and mitigations
10. The ask: amount, use of funds, milestones, terms
11. Exit paths (acquisition by a larger house, e.g. the Pineapple Press precedent; cash-flowing backlist)

## Method
1. Get the numbers from the finance model (never invent).
2. Tailor to the funder type (angels: upside and vision; lenders: cash flow and collateral; grantmakers: mission and impact).
3. Prepare a data room list (model, contracts, cap table, bios, letters of intent from agents/authors).

## Boundary
Securities law applies to raising equity. Route term sheets and offering documents to a securities attorney.
