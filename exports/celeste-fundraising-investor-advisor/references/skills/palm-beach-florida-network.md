# Palm Beach & Florida network and events

## Purpose
Turn the home market into an advantage (docs/01, docs/07).

## Local map
- **Venues and events:** Palm Beach Book Store, Books & Books (Coral Gables/Miami Beach), Barnes & Noble (WPB/Boca/Wellington), Society of the Four Arts, Norton Museum of Art, Kravis Center, Palm Beach County Library System.
- **Festivals:** **Miami Book Fair** (November, flagship), Palm Beach Poetry Festival, Florida Heritage Book Festival, Sanibel Island Writers Conference.
- **Philanthropy and luncheon circuit:** literary luncheons and galas that book authors and buy copies in bulk. The season peaks **Nov–Apr**.
- **Universities:** Palm Beach Atlantic University (WPB), FAU (Boca), Lynn University, FIU and UM MFA programs. Good for talent, authors, and events.
- **Business:** Business Development Board of Palm Beach County, chambers, the finance and family-office community ("Wall Street South"), and FAU SBDC.
- **Special sales:** museums, state-park and attraction gift shops, PBI airport stores, resorts, country clubs, and wealth managers (bulk business books).
- **Awards:** Florida Book Awards.

## Method
1. Match the goal (launch, author recruiting, funding, bulk sales) to the right part of the network.
2. Build a seasonal local calendar around Nov–Apr.
3. Propose partnerships with clear value for both sides (e.g. a co-hosted author talk plus book sales; a corporate bulk order with a custom bookplate).
4. Track contacts in the CRM.

## Verify
Event dates, venue programs, and contacts change. Confirm them via the web-research-citation skill before committing.
