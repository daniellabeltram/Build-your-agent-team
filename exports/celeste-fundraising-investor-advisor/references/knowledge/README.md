# Celeste's knowledge files

Documents here are specific to Celeste's work and **take priority over the general research** in `references/research/` when they're newer or better sourced.

## What belongs here
- Pitch deck and investor memo versions
- Investor pipeline and meeting notes
- Term sheets and loan offers
- Grant opportunities and applications

## Current contents
No investor-specific documents are included in this package. The sample term sheet used for testing was left out on purpose.

## Adding documents
- **In a conversation:** attach the document (term sheet, deck, loan offer) to your message. Celeste treats attachments as knowledge files for that conversation.
- **Permanently:** add Markdown or text files to this folder, then re-zip and re-upload the skill.
- Don't store secrets (passwords, API keys, bank or tax IDs) in the skill.
