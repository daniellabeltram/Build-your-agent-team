# 08 — Biggest Risks

[← Index](README.md)

Ranked by **likelihood × impact** for a new independent trade publisher in West Palm Beach.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Undercapitalization / cash-flow crunch.** Advances and printing are paid up front, distributors pay 60–120 days late, returns reserves are held back, and payback takes 3–5 years | High | Severe | Raise 18–24 months of runway plus a buffer. Stage title commitments. Use POD and short print runs. Keep a returns reserve in the model |
| 2 | **Hit dependency / title failure.** Most books don't earn out. One flop in a 4-title year hurts | High | High | Build a portfolio. Cap advances relative to projected sales (P&L every title before signing). Build evergreen backlist (regional, reference) |
| 3 | **Distribution access.** Top distributors require a track record or volume. A weak distributor means no bookstore presence | Medium–High | High | Start with IngramSpark or a smaller distributor. Grow to 10+ titles. Approach IPS, PGW, IPG, Simon & Schuster Distribution or PRH Publisher Services with a 3-season plan |
| 4 | **Returns.** Books are fully returnable. 20–35% returns can erase a season's profit | High | Medium–High | Conservative print runs, sell-through tracking, non-returnable terms for special sales, reprint quickly instead of overprinting |
| 5 | **Amazon concentration and margin pressure** | High | Medium | Diversify: indies, libraries, special sales, D2C. Manage Amazon discounts and ads carefully |
| 6 | **Acquisitions disadvantage.** Agents prefer the Big Five for bigger advances and reach | High | Medium | Target under-served mid-list and debut authors. Offer speed, attention, better ebook royalties and transparency. Build a niche reputation |
| 7 | **Key-person risk.** The editorial director's relationships *are* the asset | Medium | High | Equity or retention incentives, documented processes, a second editor by Year 2 |
| 8 | **AI disruption.** Content glut, AI-generated manuscripts and translations, copyright uncertainty, and pressure to license backlist to AI firms | Medium–High | Medium | AI-disclosure warranties in author contracts. Curated "human-made" brand. Deliberate policy on AI-training licensing (a revenue opportunity *and* a reputational risk) |
| 9 | **Florida book-challenge / political climate** (HB 1069; *PRH v. Gibson* struck its removal provisions in Aug 2025, but appeals and new bills are possible) | Medium | Medium (children's/YA) / Low (adult) | Prioritize adult lists first. Media-liability insurance. Join publisher and industry coalitions. Maintain national (not only FL) sales |
| 10 | **Legal liability.** Libel (memoir and business books naming real people), copyright infringement, unlicensed permissions | Medium | High | Legal vetting of risky manuscripts. Strong author warranties and indemnities. **Media liability insurance** |
| 11 | **Hybrid/vanity confusion.** Local market (e.g. author-funded hybrids) blurs the brand. The temptation to take author money for cash flow | Medium | Medium | Clear traditional positioning. If you add a services arm, use a **separate brand** with an ethical, transparent pricing model |
| 12 | **Hurricane and climate risk** (South Florida). Office, inventory, staff disruption, insurance costs | Medium (seasonal) | Medium | Don't warehouse in WPB (use the distributor's warehouse). Cloud-based ops. Business-interruption insurance. Remote-capable team |
| 13 | **Commercial real estate and cost of living rising** in WPB (Class A rents are about $48–72/sf; housing costs are up) | Medium | Low–Medium | Class B or coworking. Remote-first. Recruit people who are already local |
| 14 | **Print supply chain.** Paper price spikes, limited US printer capacity, freight | Medium | Medium | Relationships with 2–3 printers (US + overseas for color). Lock schedules early. POD as a backup |
| 15 | **Seasonality.** Q4 holiday concentration, plus a snowbird season locally | High | Low–Medium | Plan cash for Q1–Q3. Time Florida-interest titles to the Oct–Nov launch season |

## Top 3 to tackle first
1. **Capital plan** with a realistic title P&L model, including returns and payment timing (Finance agent).
2. **Distribution strategy and path to a full-service distributor** (Sales/Distribution agent).
3. **Editorial director hire plus a first-list acquisition strategy** (Editorial agent).
