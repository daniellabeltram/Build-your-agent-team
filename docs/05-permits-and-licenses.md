# 05 — Permits and Licenses

[← Index](README.md)

Publishing is a **lightly regulated** business. There is no state license to "be a publisher." The work is
mainly entity formation, local business tax, sales tax, employment registrations, and protecting IP.
*Not legal advice. Have a Florida business attorney and a CPA confirm these before filing.*

## Checklist

| # | Requirement | Agency | Cost (est.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Entity formation** (LLC or corporation) | Florida Division of Corporations, **Sunbiz.org** | ~$125 LLC / ~$70 corp | Check name availability on Sunbiz. Annual report due Jan 1–May 1 (~$138.75 LLC; $400 late fee) |
| 2 | **Fictitious name (DBA)** for each imprint name not identical to the entity | Sunbiz | ~$50 each, renew every 5 years | Must be advertised once in a local newspaper before registering |
| 3 | **EIN** | IRS | Free | Needed for banking, payroll and distributor contracts |
| 4 | **Palm Beach County Local Business Tax Receipt (BTR)** | PBC Constitutional Tax Collector (pbctax.gov) | Small, varies by classification | **Required even for one-person and home-based businesses** *(sourced)*. Renew every year by Sept 30 |
| 5 | **City of West Palm Beach Business Tax Receipt** | WPB Development Services, Business Tax | Varies | Required *in addition to* the county BTR for businesses inside city limits. Home-based businesses also need a **Home Occupation Affidavit** *(sourced)* |
| 6 | **Zoning / Certificate of Use** | City of WPB Zoning (or PBC Zoning if unincorporated, Form 103 for home occupation) | Varies | Confirm that the office use is permitted. Storing inventory or a warehouse may trigger different zoning |
| 7 | **Florida sales & use tax registration** (Form DR-1) | Florida Dept. of Revenue | Free | Needed if selling books **directly** to Florida consumers (website, events). FL rate is 6% + **Palm Beach County 1% surtax = 7%** (verify the current surtax). Sales to resellers are exempt with their resale certificate. Collect resale certificates from bookstores |
| 8 | **Out-of-state sales tax** (economic nexus) | Other states' DORs | Varies | D2C sales above state thresholds (often $100k) create obligations. Use Shopify Tax / Avalara |
| 9 | **Reemployment (unemployment) tax** | Florida DOR (RT-6) | Rate-based | Once you have employees |
| 10 | **Workers' compensation** | Private carrier | Varies | Required in FL for non-construction employers with **4+ employees** |
| 11 | **New-hire reporting, I-9, E-Verify** | FL New Hire Reporting Center; USCIS | Free | **Florida requires E-Verify for private employers with 25+ employees** (2023 law, SB 1718) |
| 12 | **Federal payroll** (941, W-2, 1099-NEC for freelancers and authors' royalties via 1099-MISC) | IRS | — | **Royalties to authors are reported on 1099-MISC (Box 2)** |
| 13 | **Signage permit** | City of WPB Building | Varies | Only if you put up exterior signage |
| 14 | **Certificate of Occupancy / fire inspection** | WPB Building / Fire Rescue | Varies | Usually part of leasing a commercial space. Confirm the landlord has it |

## Publishing-specific "licenses" and IP

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **ISBNs** | Buy from **Bowker** (the only official US agency). The publisher of record is listed on the ISBN. Each format (HC, PB, ebook, audio) needs its own ISBN |
| **Barcodes (EAN-13 with price add-on)** | Required for retail. Bowker charges $25 each, or generate them free with standard tools |
| **Library of Congress** | PCN / CIP data (free; CIP is for established publishers with 3+ authors published). **Mandatory deposit:** 2 copies of published works to the US Copyright Office |
| **Copyright registration** | US Copyright Office, eCO (~$45–65 per work). Standard practice is to register in the **author's** name, handled by the publisher |
| **Trademarks** | USPTO registration of the house name and imprint names/logos (Class 16 printed matter; Class 9 ebooks/audio; Class 41 publishing services) |
| **Permissions and fair use** | Process for clearing quotes, lyrics (very expensive) and images. Keep permissions logs |
| **Contracts** | Author agreements (grant of rights, territories, formats, subsidiary rights splits, option clause, reversion, warranties and **indemnities**), freelancer work-for-hire, translation, audio license |
| **Privacy** | Florida Digital Bill of Rights mostly applies to very large companies. Still follow CAN-SPAM and good privacy policy practice for newsletters and e-commerce |
| **Accessibility** | European Accessibility Act (since June 2025) affects ebooks sold in the EU. Produce **EPUB 3 accessible** files from day one |

## Florida-specific legal context: content
- **HB 1069 (2023)** and related school-library laws let parents challenge books in **K-12 schools**.
  In **Aug 2025**, a federal judge ruled the book-removal provisions unconstitutional in
  **Penguin Random House v. Gibson** (PRH, HarperCollins, Hachette, Macmillan, S&S, Sourcebooks and the Authors Guild).
  Appeals and new legislation are possible. **This doesn't restrict publishing or retail sales**, but it does affect
  **school-library sales** of children's and YA titles in Florida. See [risks](08-risks.md).

## Suggested order of operations
1. Pick a name, then run Sunbiz and USPTO knockout searches → 2. File the LLC → 3. Get an EIN → 4. Open a bank account →
5. Sign the office lease (confirm zoning first) → 6. County BTR → 7. City BTR → 8. FL DOR sales tax registration →
9. Buy ISBNs → 10. Insurance → 11. Payroll and employer registrations before the first hire.
