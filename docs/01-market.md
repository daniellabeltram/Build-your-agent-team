**Formats (Trade):** Hardcover and paperback make up about 73% of trade revenue ($8.0–8.2B and $7.8–8.0B).
Digital (ebook plus digital audio) is about 21.5% and grew 4.2%. Ebooks are flat at about $1B a year, while
**digital audio keeps growing**.

**Monthly reporters** (a narrower publisher panel, AAP Dec 2025 StatShot): trade revenue was down 0.5% year to date
at $9.8B. Real growth is flat to slightly positive. This is a **mature, share-shifting market**, not
a growing one.

## Structural trends that matter for a new house

1. **Consolidation at the top.** The Big Five (PRH, HarperCollins, Simon & Schuster, Hachette and
   Macmillan) control most of the trade market. PRH alone is commonly estimated at about a quarter or more
   of US trade (est.). Mid-list authors are underserved, which creates an acquisition opportunity for indies.
2. **Backlist dominates.** Most unit sales at large houses come from titles more than 12 months old (est. ~60–70%).
   A new house has no backlist, which is the core economic disadvantage in the early years.
3. **Discovery has moved to social platforms.** BookTok, Bookstagram and newsletters (Substack) now
   drive breakouts, especially romance, romantasy and thrillers.
4. **Audio is the growth format.** Selling or keeping audio rights is a key revenue decision.
5. **Religious and inspirational books are growing fast.** This is relevant to Florida's large faith-based readership.
6. **AI-generated content glut** on Amazon/KDP is raising discovery costs. Human-curated imprints
   with a recognizable brand become *more* valuable.
7. **Independent bookstores have recovered.** ABA membership has grown since 2020, which helps regional and literary lists.

**What this means:**
- **Older, affluent and highly literate.** Retirees and seasonal residents are among the heaviest
  book buyers (hardcover, large print, book clubs). There is a strong **seasonal peak from November to April**.
- **Wealth and executive density.** West Palm Beach ("Wall Street South") has had heavy finance and family-office
  relocation since 2020. That means demand for business, finance, leadership and memoir titles, and many
  potential **authors with platforms and budgets**.
- **Bilingual opportunity.** Florida has a large Spanish-speaking market. Spanish-language and bilingual
  children's books are an underserved niche (PRH has Vintage Español and Penguin Random House Grupo Editorial).
- **Florida-interest books** (history, nature, Everglades, cooking, travel, hurricanes, real estate)
  sell steadily through regional retail: gift shops, museums, Publix-area bookstores and airports.
- **Literary infrastructure nearby:** Miami Book Fair (November, one of the largest in the US), Palm Beach
  literary and charity luncheon circuit, the Society of the Four Arts (Palm Beach), Kravis Center, Norton Museum,
  Florida Atlantic University (Boca Raton) and Palm Beach Atlantic University (WPB).

## Market sizing for this business (est.)

- A small independent trade publisher with **10–15 titles a year** and a good distributor typically
  earns **$0.5M–$3M a year** in net revenue once it has a backlist (3–5 years in). Breakouts are the upside.
- A realistic Year 1 with 4–6 frontlist titles: **$75k–$300k net revenue**.
