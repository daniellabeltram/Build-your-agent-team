### Unit economics: one $28.00 hardcover (est.)

| | Per copy |
|---|---|
| List price | $28.00 |
| Average trade discount to retailers/wholesalers (~50–55%) | −$14.70 |
| **Publisher gross receipts** | **$13.30** |
| Distributor fee (~25–30% of net receipts) | −$3.70 |
| Print cost (3k run) | −$4.00 |
| Royalty (10% of list) | −$2.80 |
| **Contribution per copy sold** | **≈ $2.80** |

Plus **returns** (books are returnable, and 20–35% of shipped units commonly come back), so the
net copies sold is less than the copies shipped. **Break-even on a $48k title is roughly 7,000–10,000 net hardcover copies**,
but audio, ebook (much higher margin), subsidiary rights (foreign, film/TV, book club, paperback) and
backlist years improve it significantly. *Most titles won't break even. The list works because 1–2 hits
in every 10 fund the rest.*

**Paths to better margins:** print-on-demand (no inventory, but higher unit cost), direct-to-consumer
sales, special and bulk sales, ebook and audio, rights licensing, and premium editions.

## D. Capital plan (est.)

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Titles published | 4–6 | 8–10 | 12–15 |
| Net revenue | $75k–$300k | $300k–$900k | $700k–$2M |
| Operating spend | $400k–$1M | $600k–$1.3M | $900k–$1.8M |
| **Cumulative capital need** | | | **$500k–$1.2M** (lean to moderate) |

**Cash-flow traps:** distributors pay **60–120 days after sale** and hold **returns reserves**
(often 15–25%). Printing and advances are paid up front. Seasonal concentration (Q4) means
cash troughs in Q1–Q3.

**Funding options:** founder capital, angel investors (the Palm Beach family-office ecosystem), SBA 7(a)
loan (requires collateral and a business plan), revenue-based financing (once there's a backlist),
a nonprofit sister imprint (grant-funded literary or Florida-heritage titles), and Florida/Palm Beach County
small-business incentives (e.g. Business Development Board of Palm Beach County, FAU SBDC advising).
