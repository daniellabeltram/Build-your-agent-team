# 07 — Marketing

[← Index](README.md)

A publisher markets on **three levels at once**: to the **trade** (so books get stocked), to
**readers** (so books sell through), and to **authors and agents** (so we can acquire the next list).

## 1. Trade marketing (gets books on shelves)
- **Metadata excellence:** a strong title, subtitle, BISAC codes, keywords, description and comp titles in ONIX feeds.
  Bad metadata is the #1 hidden killer of indie sales.
- **Catalogs and sell-in:** seasonal catalogs (Spring/Fall) through Edelweiss+. The distributor's sales reps pitch
  buyers **6–9 months before publication**.
- **Pre-publication reviews:** *Publishers Weekly*, *Kirkus*, *Library Journal*, *Booklist*, *Shelf Awareness*.
  Library buyers depend on these.
- **ARCs / galleys:** NetGalley and Edelweiss for reviewers, booksellers and librarians.
- **Indie bookseller outreach:** ABA Winter Institute, SIBA (Southern Independent Booksellers Alliance) Discovery Show.
  **The Indie Next List** is a big lever.
- **Library marketing:** ALA conferences, Florida Library Association, Palm Beach County Library System events.

## 2. Consumer marketing (gets books sold through)
| Tactic | Best for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Author platform building (newsletter, social) | All | Low |
| **BookTok / Bookstagram influencer seeding** | Romance, fantasy, thriller, YA | Low–medium |
| Amazon Ads + BookBub Featured Deals | Genre, backlist | Medium. Pay as you go |
| Podcast tours | Business, nonfiction, memoir | Low–medium |
| Earned media and publicity | Nonfiction, upmarket fiction | Medium (publicist) |
| Goodreads giveaways | All | Low |
| Book club kits / reading guides | Upmarket fiction, memoir | Low |
| Price promotions on ebooks | Backlist revival | Low |

## 3. Local Palm Beach and Florida playbook
- **Launch events:** Palm Beach Book Store, Books & Books, Barnes & Noble (CityPlace/Rosemary Square area), the
  Society of the Four Arts, Norton Museum, Kravis Center talks.
- **Charity luncheon circuit:** Palm Beach's literary luncheons and nonprofit galas regularly book authors and
  can bulk-buy copies.
- **Media:** *Palm Beach Post*, *Palm Beach Daily News* ("the Shiny Sheet"), *Palm Beach Illustrated*,
  *Boca Raton* magazine, WPTV/WPBF, WLRN (NPR Miami), the *Sun Sentinel*, the *Miami Herald*.
- **Festivals:** **Miami Book Fair** (November, flagship), Sanibel Island Writers Conference, Florida Heritage Book Festival,
  Palm Beach Poetry Festival, SunFest-adjacent events.
- **Special sales:** Florida museums, state parks gift shops, airports (PBI Hudson stores), resorts, country clubs,
  wealth-management firms (bulk business books as client gifts).
- **Awards:** Florida Book Awards (FSU), IPPY, Foreword INDIES, IBPA Benjamin Franklin Awards. Medals help sell-in.

## 4. House brand and acquisitions marketing
- Position the house in Publishers Lunch / Publishers Marketplace **deal announcements**. Every acquisition is PR.
- Agent outreach: individual lunches (NYC trips twice a year), "what we're looking for" newsletters for agents, and attending
  the **Frankfurt and London Book Fairs** once the rights list is ready.
- Founder thought leadership: LinkedIn and podcasts on "a new kind of publisher in Florida."
- Transparent author experience (a portal with real-time sales and quarterly royalties) as a selling point.

## 5. Budget guidance (est.)
- House-level brand and trade marketing: **$15k–$50k a year** in Years 1–2.
- Per-title marketing: **$2k–$25k**, weighted toward lead titles (one or two "lead" titles per season get most of the budget).
- Rule of thumb: plan to spend **10–15% of a title's projected net revenue** on marketing, with more for lead titles.

## KPIs for the marketing agent to track
- Pre-order units and initial sell-in (ship) by account
- Sell-through rate and return rate by title
- Review coverage count (trade + consumer)
- Newsletter list size and open rate. Social followers by imprint
- Cost per acquisition on paid channels. BookBub ROI
- Agent submissions per month (a proxy for brand strength)
