# Expert Roster: Agent Team for a West Palm Beach Publishing House

[← Index](README.md)

The expert advisors a consultant needs for launching an independent, PRH-style trade publisher in West Palm Beach, FL. Each role is meant to become one agent. Roles and skills come from the research in this folder ([01](01-market.md)–[08](08-risks.md)).

**How to read this:** Part 1 lists the roles (agents), each with a one-line job description. Part 2 defines the skills, each with the agents that use it. Many skills are shared, so you build each skill once and attach it to several agents. Part 3 is the reverse view: each agent's skill set.

**Totals:** 25 agents, 35 skills.

## Build notes

- **Build the most widely shared skills first.** They unlock the most agents: Web research & source citation (8 agents); Publishing contract drafting & review (7 agents); Customer segmentation & personas (6 agents); Title P&L & unit economics (6 agents); Publishing industry data synthesis (5 agents); Cash-flow & capital planning (5 agents).
- **A01 Engagement Coordinator** is the consultant's single point of contact. It routes questions and reconciles the other agents' advice.
- **Suggested first wave**, matching the top-3 risks in [08-risks.md](08-risks.md): A15 Finance & Capital Planner, A06 Sales & Distribution Manager, A04 Editorial & Acquisitions Director, plus A01 to coordinate them.
- **Optional consolidation** for a smaller team: merge A05 into A04, A07 into A06, A10 into A08, A16 into A15, A19 into A18, A23 and A24 into A21, and A13 into A14. That leaves a 16-agent core.
- **Legal agents advise; they don't replace counsel.** The legal agents (A18–A20) should flag issues for review by a licensed Florida attorney.
