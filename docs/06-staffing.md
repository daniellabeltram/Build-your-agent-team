## Salary benchmarks
- **PW 2025 Salary & Jobs Report:** median pay for all respondents rose $5,000, to **$80,000** *(sourced)*.
- **PW 2026 Report:** average pay for people with **≤6 years of experience was $57,100** in 2025 (up from $56,000) *(sourced)*.
- Publishing pays below other creative and media industries. Florida's lower cost of living (compared with NYC) plus
  remote or hybrid flexibility is a **recruiting advantage**. Florida has **no state income tax**.

## Phased hiring plan

### Phase 0: pre-launch (months 0–6), 1–2 people
- **Founder / Publisher:** strategy, capital, first acquisitions.
- **Editorial Director** (the first critical hire): brings agent relationships and credibility.
- **Freelance bench:** copyeditors, proofreaders, cover designers, typesetter, publicist, entertainment/publishing attorney, CPA with royalty experience.

### Phase 1: first list (Year 1, 4–6 titles), 3–4 FTE
- + **Managing Editor / Production** (could be contract)
- + **Marketing & Publicity Manager** (one person to start)
- Distribution, warehousing and sales reps are **outsourced to the distributor**.

### Phase 2: scaling (Years 2–3, 8–15 titles), 6–9 FTE
- + 1–2 Acquiring Editors (one per imprint)
- + Editorial Assistant(s) (often local university grads: PBAU, FAU, Lynn University)
- + Sales & Rights Manager
- + Digital Marketing / Social Coordinator
- + Operations / Royalties Coordinator

## Talent sources
- **NYC publishing diaspora:** many experienced editors left NYC after 2020 for remote work, and some are in Florida.
- **Columbia, NYU and Denver publishing institutes** for entry-level hires.
- **Local:** FAU (Boca Raton), PBAU (WPB), Lynn University, FIU, University of Miami MFA.
- Job boards: PW Jobzone, Bookjobs.com, Publishers Lunch job board, LinkedIn, Indeed.
- Industry groups: IBPA, Florida Authors & Publishers Association (FAPA), Women's National Book Association.

## Org design notes
- Remote-first with a small WPB hub keeps costs low and widens the talent pool.
- Each imprint needs an **"owner" editor** with taste and agent relationships. This is the core asset of a publishing house.
- Outsource anything that isn't taste or relationships (distribution, printing, warehousing, IT, payroll).
