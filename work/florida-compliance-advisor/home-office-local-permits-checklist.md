# Home-office launch checklist: City of West Palm Beach + Palm Beach County

Prepared by: florida-compliance-advisor | Research date: 2026-11-20 | Status: advisory, confirm with a Florida business attorney/CPA.

**Assumed facts:** Florida LLC; office in the founder's residence inside WPB city limits; 1–2 people working on site; no walk-in customers; no inventory at home (distributor ships); occasional direct online sales to Florida consumers.

**Research limitation:** Direct page fetches of wpb.org, pbctax.gov and flsenate.gov were blocked in this session (hosts not on the network allowlist). Facts below come from search-engine extracts of those official pages and PDFs. Every fee marked "to verify" must be confirmed by phone/email before filing.

**Order logic:** Sunbiz → EIN → (FN) → DOR can run in parallel with step 0. The **City BTR comes before the County BTR** because the County application needs the city's zoning sign-off (Section 2). Don't start operating until both receipts are issued.

## Home-occupation rules that apply (WPB)

Florida's home-based business law, **F.S. 559.955 (2021)**, sets an upper limit on how far local governments can regulate a home business. The WPB Code text (Ch. 94 zoning) could not be retrieved, so treat the city-specific items as **to verify**.

| Topic | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Employees on site | Residents plus **up to 2 non-resident employees/contractors** working on site; any number of remote workers | F.S. 559.955 (sourced, 2021 Senate analysis). Some third-party summaries of the WPB code show stricter older limits; the state law controls if it conflicts (to verify with the city) |
| Clients / visitors | Not an issue (no walk-ins). Keep author/agent meetings off-site or occasional | est. |
| Parking | Business parking can't exceed what a similar residence would normally generate; vehicles go in legal spaces off the right-of-way | F.S. 559.955 (sourced) |
| Signage | Local sign rules apply. Plan **no exterior business sign**; the mailbox/address only | F.S. 559.955 + WPB sign code (to verify) |
| Appearance | From the street, the home must keep its residential look | F.S. 559.955 (sourced) |
| Inventory | None at home. If you later store book stock or ship from home, re-check zoning and the affidavit | docs/05; est. |
| HOA / deed restrictions | F.S. 559.955 does **not** override HOA or condo declarations (to verify subsection) | to verify |

## Other address types (1–2 lines)

- **Unincorporated Palm Beach County:** You need the County BTR only. For a residential home business that isn't a storage or landscaping business, no zoning approval is required (pbctax.gov). The older PBC Zoning Form 103 appears to be legacy (to verify). The 559.955 limits still apply.
- **Another municipality** (e.g., Royal Palm Beach, Lake Park, Palm Beach Gardens, Town of Palm Beach): Apply for that city's own BTR and get its zoning sign-off first, then the County BTR. Forms, fees and affidavits vary by city.

## Compliance calendar

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Jan 1–May 1 | Sunbiz LLC annual report ($138.75) |
| Monthly/quarterly/annual (DOR-assigned) | Florida sales tax return (DR-15), filed even if $0 |
| Jul 1–Sep 30 | Renew the City BTR and the County BTR |
| Every 5 years | Fictitious name renewal |
| On any change of address or of home-occupation facts | Update City BTR ($25 change-of-location fee on the 2025 form, to verify) and County BTR |

## Tax-rate note (corrects docs/05)

Palm Beach County's combined sales tax rate is **6.5% from Jan 1, 2026**: 6% state + 0.5% school capital outlay surtax, which runs through Dec 31, 2035. The 1% infrastructure surtax was repealed effective Dec 31, 2025 (FL DOR TIP 25A01-15; DR-15DSS 2026). docs/05 still shows 7%.
